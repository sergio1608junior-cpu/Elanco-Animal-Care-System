Passerine tick (Dermacentor frontalis)

Primary Hosts: Passerine birds, wild mammals, dogs

Habitat: Woodlands, hedgerows, scrubby vegetation

Prevention Tips: Regular anti-parasitic treatment, check pet coats after walks.
